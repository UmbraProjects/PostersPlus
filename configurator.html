What's new
v1.2.0
  • Anime-native posters through AniList and Kitsu, with separate anime rating weights on the Weights tab
  • New 16:9 landscape layout, poster-coloured vignettes, and frosted bars and notches that match the vignette tint
  • IMDb ids are optional: titles TMDB has no IMDb link for now render with ratings and sashes instead of failing
  • QualiCache quality source, and BluRay/WEBRip answers now show the Web badge instead of nothing
  • MDBList-free rating inputs: TMDB's own score and the IMDb dataset, as a primary source or a fallback when MDBList has no value
  • Background cache warming for trending, popular, and custom-catalog titles, now quota-aware so a free MDBList key isn't drained in one cycle
  • Festival sashes only name a top prize the film actually won, checked against verified winner lists
  • Award sash labels read "Globe Winner" / "Globe Nominee", with an optional star icon in place of the text; 78th Emmy winners added
  • Ten new presets, row tooltips, a title-link menu, and generated URLs about a third as long
  • Brazilian Portuguese translations, and the remaining sash vocabulary translated in every language
v1.1.0
  • New Frosted Bar poster layout, with frosted, black, silver, gold, and rating-focused styles
  • Rebuilt textless-poster detection around a new OCR model, with background scanning and load controls for live installations
  • Smarter TMDB poster selection: a tiny number of votes can no longer promote a badly rated poster over substantially better artwork
  • Expanded artwork selection: original-art controls, language-aware poster matching, improved logo fallback, and face- and saliency-aware cropping
  • New notch sash style, plus release-status sashes for BluRay, Streaming, Cinema, and Production
  • Backdrop fallback and genre-tinted fallback canvas when no poster or textless art is available
  • MDBList rate-limit cooldown: automatic global pause on 429 to prevent cascade failures
  • New preset gallery and a reorganized, mobile-friendly tabbed configurator
  • New poster-output translations for French, Portuguese, and Italian
